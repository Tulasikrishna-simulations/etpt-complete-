## The theory in three sentences

Technology direction is structurally determined by a Bio-Perception Tensor **B⊗P** — the outer product of what a mind can *do* and what it can *perceive*; intensity follows a saturating function of survival pressure, and the two decouple. The framework predicts a reclassification threshold **ρ_c ≈ 150 nodes/cm² (≡ 3×10⁴ bits/cm²/s)** above which haptic input on plastic cortex becomes genuine spatial perception — a new sense for blind humans, not vision restored. It specifies its own single falsifiable break-point (direct neural interface) and, tested across substrates, holds for a transformer-based vision system as well as for biology — suggesting a theory of information-processing minds as such.

**Governing equation:** T_path = (B ⊗ P) · D · (1 − e^(−αS))

## Honest status

The framework is internally consistent, falsifiable, and grounded in existing literature (Sadato, Pascual-Leone, Amedi on cortical recruitment; Bach-y-Rita, BrainPort on substitution; Beauchamp & Yoshor on direct cortical form-vision). **No experiment has been run yet** — every parameter is a careful estimate, and the cross-substrate result is consistency, not yet a confirmed prediction. This is a program of measurement that has not yet measured. The next step is the experiment, and it is specified.
